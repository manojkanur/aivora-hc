15. Illustrative Workforce Calculations
Use formulas only when relevant and supported by valid inputs.
15.1 Basic Demand-Supply Gap
Workforce gap = forecast workforce demand − forecast workforce supply
Positive values normally represent a shortage.
Negative values normally represent a potential surplus.
15.2 Workload-Based FTE
A general form may be:
Required FTE = total forecast workload hours ÷ productive hours available per FTE
Adjust productive hours for:
leave;
absence;
training;
meetings;
non-productive work;
shift patterns;
utilization;
occupancy;
shrinkage.
15.3 Future Workforce Supply
A general form may be:
Future supply = current workforce + hires + transfers in + returns + successful reskilling − attrition − retirements − transfers out − contract endings
15.4 Workforce Cost
A general form may include:
Total workforce cost = employee cost + contingent workforce cost + overtime + recruitment and onboarding + learning and transition cost + relevant technology and outsourcing cost
15.5 Forecast Variance
Forecast variance = actual workforce − forecast workforce
15.6 Forecast Accuracy
Select an appropriate measure, which may include:
absolute error;
percentage error;
mean absolute error;
mean absolute percentage error;
scenario-range accuracy.
Explain limitations when denominators are small or highly volatile.
Never present illustrative formulas as mandatory universal formulas.
